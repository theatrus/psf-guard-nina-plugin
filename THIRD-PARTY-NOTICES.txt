License: Mozilla Public License 2.0 (MPL-2.0).

The full MPL-2.0 text is also included in this archive as LICENSE.

PSF Guard Sync's database compatibility work and test fixtures are based on
Target Scheduler's database definitions and behavior: projects, targets,
exposure templates and plans, acquired images, grading and accepted-count
semantics, thumbnails, and flat-history coverage. Thank you to Tom Palmer
and the contributors who built and maintain that foundation.

Database schema and migrations:
https://github.com/tcpalmer/nina.plugin.targetscheduler/tree/main/NINA.Plugin.TargetScheduler/Database

Target Scheduler itself is installed separately; no Target Scheduler plugin
assembly is bundled in this archive. These credits describe the software
and database-format provenance, not ownership or licensing of the user's
catalog entries, observing metadata, or image files. Synchronization does
not place that user data under MPL-2.0.
